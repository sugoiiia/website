ARVP: Front Panel

ARVP  ·  Mechanical

Structural front panel that rigidly ties both frame halves together while consolidating two cameras and a self-waterproofed OLED display.

Gallery
Problem
  • The frame was in two separate halves, each carrying its own camera, with nothing rigidly connecting them at the front
  • During jerky rolls the two halves could shift relative to each other, which knocked the thrusters out of alignment, so the bot no longer thrust where it was calibrated to
  • The front also needed an OLED display, and everything up front had to survive underwater
Proposed Solution
  • Design one front panel that ties both frame halves together into a single rigid front face, so the frame moves as one body and the thrusters stay aligned through hard maneuvers
  • Use that same panel to consolidate both cameras and a waterproofed OLED, since the structural piece was already there
Design Process
  • Designed the panel as a structural member that bridges and closes the previously open front of the frame, locking the two halves together so they can't shift relative to each other during rolls
  • Waterproofed the OLED myself using a combination of epoxy, 3D printing, ethernet cables, and gasket sealant to keep water out of the display
  • Consolidated the two cameras and the sealed OLED into that same panel, so one part serves as the front face, the display housing, and a structural tie
Final Product
  • A single front panel that rigidly joins both frame halves, keeping the thrusters aligned through the jerky motion that used to offset them
  • Same panel carries two cameras and a self-waterproofed OLED, doing structural, sensing, and display duty in one part
